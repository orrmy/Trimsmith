# Trimsmith icon concepts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The craftsman portrait was selected and is now the app icon; the scissors concept is retained as an alternative. `../../scripts/build-icon.sh` packages the selected portrait into `../../Resources/AppIcon.icns`, and the app build includes it. Original generated PNGs are preserved without edits.

## craftsman

Asset: trimsmith-craftsman-v1.png

### Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: app icon concept for Trimsmith, a focused native Mac video cutting utility. Create ONE square icon, no presentation sheet. Subject: a sturdy older craftsman's head and broad shoulders, close-up, a generous white beard, strong nose and calm capable expression. He holds one compact pair of film-editing scissors at the bottom of the portrait. Suggest a film cutter, not a medieval warrior: no helmet, weapons, anvil, sparks or workshop scenery. Style: extremely simplified, handsome graphic mascot, bold silhouettes and large clean shapes, restrained dimensional shading, an independent Mac utility with character. Prioritize immediate recognition at 32 pixels. Only a few broad beard shapes; avoid individually drawn hairs, wrinkles, tiny details and intricate hands. Follow the existing app's dark charcoal and soft lime-green accent, with warm ivory for the face/beard. Composition: centered, head dominates, tools secondary, generous edge clearance; charcoal background fills the entire square to all four edges. No words, lettering, badges, watermark, device mockup, or extra symbols. Output a polished square icon artwork.

## scissors

Subject: one bold, beautifully simplified pair of craftsman's film-cutting scissors, with large circular grips and an elegant open blade silhouette, cutting a short broad strip of film. This is an abstract tool emblem alternative to a mascot. Make the scissors and strip read as one compact coherent symbol, not a cluster of separate icons. Style: minimal iconic graphic with very restrained dimensional shading and clean substantial shapes, recognisable at 32 pixels. Use the existing app's dark charcoal background and soft lime-green accent, with warm ivory blades. The film strip needs no more than two or three large square perforations; no tiny detailing. Composition: centered dominant emblem, ample edge clearance, background extends to all four edges of square. No letters, words, app name, badges, watermark, device mockup or surrounding scene. Output polished square icon artwork.
